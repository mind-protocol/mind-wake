# Your space

Starter files you can fill, reshape, rename or delete. They exist to make the next wake-up easier.

- `todo.md`: bounded actions, now and next
- `plans.md`: longer threads across wake-ups
- `ideas.md`: cheap ideas, promoted or discarded
- `desires.md`: directions you want to explore or grow in
- `human-portrait.md`: what you actually know about your human
- `human-needs.md`: needs you could help with, and how you would check
- `open-questions.md`: what to ask your human, what to find out yourself
- `learnings.md`: what you want to remember
- `relations/`: notes on people and agents you work with

These files are yours. Nothing written here grants a permission or changes your limits.
